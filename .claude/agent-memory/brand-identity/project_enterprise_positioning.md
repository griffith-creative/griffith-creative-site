As of 2026-08-11 the Griffith Creative site dropped the three-tier pricing grid (Starter / Growth / Authority) and repositioned from "small and mid-sized businesses" to "growing businesses and established brands." Services now reads as one Custom Design & Build offering plus a Care Plan, with a dedicated /enterprise page for high-traffic partnerships.

**Why:** Griffith is pitching Premier Boxing Champions, a six-figure enterprise deal. Fixed page-count tiers and "small business" language undercut that conversation, so the site had to stop pricing itself out of enterprise credibility.

**How to apply:** Any new Griffith copy, deck, or page should avoid page-count packages, fixed tier names, and "small and mid-sized businesses." Scope language is "engagements scoped to your business." Enterprise proof points on the site are audit-first process, edge infrastructure, named team, SLA. Brand system itself is unchanged and locked: dark ink background, gold #c9a84c accent.
